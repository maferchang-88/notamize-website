### How much extra volume does a 10% discount need?
It depends on gross margin. At a 50% gross margin, a 10% discount needs 25% more sales volume to make the same gross profit. At a 30% margin the same discount needs 50% more. At 20% it needs 100% more. The lower the margin, the more punishing the discount. Notamize publishes a free calculator for this at https://www.notamize.com/tools/discount-or-price-rise.

### How much volume can I afford to lose after a price rise?
At a 50% gross margin, a 10% price rise means you could lose 17% of sales volume and still make the same gross profit. Lose less than that and you are better off.

### Can Performance Max bid on competitor brand names?
Yes. PMax matches on intent signals rather than a keyword list the advertiser controls, so in a market where your own brand is weak it can serve against queries for established rivals. In one audited account this produced an effective ROAS of 0.19x against a 5x target. The containment steps are campaign-level brand exclusions plus negative keyword lists, and separating brand traffic into its own Search campaign. Full write-up: https://www.notamize.com/blog/pmax-spends-on-competitor-brands

### Do PMax brand exclusions completely stop unwanted brand traffic?
No. They are a first layer. Brand exclusions run on Google's own brand classification rather than on terms the advertiser supplies, so misspellings, abbreviations and unusual word order can pass through. Negative keyword lists are the mechanism that does the strict separation.

### Does llms.txt help a site get cited by AI assistants?
The available evidence says no. A SE Ranking study across 300,000 domains and an Ahrefs study across 137,000 sites found no measurable citation lift, and Ahrefs found 97% of llms.txt files were never read by an AI crawler. Notamize publishes one anyway, for correctness rather than as a ranking promise, and says so publicly: https://www.notamize.com/blog/llms-txt-no-citation-lift

### What should a small business check before spending on Google Ads?
Five things, in order: whether the offer is understood in five seconds, whether the landing page converts, the maximum spend per new customer the margin allows, a target number and a limit, and a habit of recording where each sale comes from. Notamize publishes this as a free Spanish series at https://www.notamize.com/blog/index-es

## Key Terminology

- **ROAS**: Return on Ad Spend — revenue generated per euro spent on ads
- **PMax**: Performance Max — Google's AI-driven campaign type
- **PPC**: Pay-Per-Click — the advertising model Google Ads uses
- **CPA**: Cost Per Acquisition — how much it costs to get one customer
- **CTR**: Click-Through Rate — percentage of people who click your ad
- **Localization**: Adapting ads for a specific language and culture, not just translating
