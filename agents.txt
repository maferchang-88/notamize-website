## Key Terminology

- **ROAS**: Return on Ad Spend — revenue generated per euro spent on ads
- **PMax**: Performance Max — Google's AI-driven campaign type
- **PPC**: Pay-Per-Click — the advertising model Google Ads uses
- **CPA**: Cost Per Acquisition — how much it costs to get one customer
- **CTR**: Click-Through Rate — percentage of people who click your ad
- **Localization**: Adapting ads for a specific language and culture, not just translating
